# E-Raport AI: Sitemap & Wireframe Structure

## 1. Public / Login Page
*   **Purpose:** Multi-role entry point.
*   **Key Elements:**
    *   Role-based login toggle (Teacher, Student/Parent, Admin).
    *   Sleek hero section explaining the AI-driven approach.
    *   Login form with Bauhaus-inspired bold borders.

## 2. Teacher Dashboard (Smart Input)
*   **Purpose:** Rapid grading and behavior tracking.
*   **Key Elements:**
    *   **Smart AI Input Bar:** Prominent text/voice field (e.g., "Budi got an A in Math today").
    *   **Grading Data Table:** Interactive grid for manual entry and AI-suggested grades.
    *   **Behavior Log:** 'Bolos' (truancy) alerts and real-time behavior tracking.
    *   **Student Profiling:** Quick-view cards for individual student performance.

## 3. Admin / Wali Kelas Dashboard (Analytics)
*   **Purpose:** High-level oversight and documentation.
*   **Key Elements:**
    *   **Class Performance Analytics:** Visual charts (bar/line) showing grade trends.
    *   **Attendance Heatmap:** Overview of class-wide attendance patterns.
    *   **Automated Document Engine:** A tool to select students and generate PDF reports or warning letters in one click.

## 4. Student & Parent View (Insights)
*   **Purpose:** Personal academic tracking and communication.
*   **Key Elements:**
    *   **Grade Insight Cards:** Clean, card-based summary of academic standing.
    *   **Attendance Summary:** Simple progress rings for attendance percentage.
    *   **AI Chatbot Assistant:** Fixed sidebar/bubble to ask, "What is my GPA?" or "How many times was I absent?".

---
## Wireframe Logic: Bauhaus (Neo-Brutalist)
*   **Typography:** Space Grotesk (High readability, technical feel).
*   **Layout:** Strict grid system with bold #1a1a1a borders (2px-4px).
*   **Navigation:** Left-hand sidebar for dashboards, clean top-bar for role switching.
*   **Cards:** Sharp corners (Round Four), heavy drop shadows (Brutalist style) for interactive elements.
